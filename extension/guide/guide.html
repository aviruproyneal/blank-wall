What Blank Wall is

Blank Wall gives you a quiet pause before a site you have blocked. When you open that site, the wall appears instead. You can wait, write a short note, or record a habit pattern, then decide what to do next.

Quick start

  1. Open the popup and click Block this site on a page you want to pause before opening.
  2. Open the dashboard to set a wall duration and, if you want, a reminder note or counter site.
  3. Visit the blocked site. The wall appears with a countdown.
  4. When the countdown ends, choose Continue, Counter site, or Leave.

The wall

The timer sits in the top-right. Any reminder note sits in the top-left. The note and habit icons sit at the bottom-left while the timer is running. When time is up, Continue returns to the site and grants the allowance set for it. Counter site opens your chosen alternative instead. Leave closes the tab. The wall reads its settings from the saved site, not the wall URL. Reloading the wall resumes the same countdown; it does not start a second wall.

Per-site settings

On the Blocked sites page, edit any site. Set the wall duration, a short reminder note, and a counter site. Choose whether Continue is available and, when it is, how many minutes its allowance lasts. Blocking a hostname also covers its subdomains; the popup shows which entry applies.

Notes

Use Quick note on the wall for what you were about to do or how you felt. Notes are saved with a time and site. Review, edit, search, and delete them on the Notes page.

Habit patterns

A pattern has four parts: Trigger (what happened first), Response (what you did), Reward (what you got from it), and Counter-plan (what you could do instead). For example:

Trigger: bored at my desk.

Response: opened Instagram.

Reward: quick distraction.

Counter-plan: stand up and stretch for two minutes.

When you use an existing pattern on the wall, you can check “I used my counter-plan” and the occurrence records it.

Your data and backup

Everything stays in this browser. Where supported, you can connect a backup folder to keep Markdown copies of notes and habits. Export everything saves a JSON snapshot, and Import restores notes, habits, and sites from the folder or the JSON file. Import skips malformed records and reports how many were imported and skipped. Before it replaces anything, it downloads a JSON safety snapshot.

Tips

Start with short walls and raise them slowly. Pick a counter site you actually want to visit. Review your patterns once a week.
